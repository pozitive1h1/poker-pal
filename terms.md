## 1. What the app is

Poker Pal is a study and practice tool for No-Limit Texas Hold'em. It trains you to estimate your chances and to choose an action, and explains each answer with references to poker books.

- **No real-money gambling.** The app has no betting, no chips that can be bought or exchanged, and no prizes.
- **Not a real-time assistant.** The app is meant for study away from the table. Many poker sites and live rooms forbid software that helps you during a hand. You are responsible for following the rules of any game, site or room where you play. Do not use the app in a way those rules prohibit.
- **No guarantee of results.** The advice is a training model based on simplified ranges and published poker theory. It may be wrong for a particular situation and does not guarantee winnings. Poker involves risk; play responsibly and only where it is legal for you.
