# Poker Pal — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Poker Pal ("the app") is a poker study trainer for iPhone made by Alexander Maximov ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: **we do not collect any personal data.** The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we run no servers that receive your data.

## What stays on your iPhone

- **Training progress and settings** — your answers, sessions, level, steps and preferences are stored on your device.
- **Calculator history** — the hands you calculate are stored on your device.
- **Daily free-use counters** — how many screenshot readings or live sessions you used today, kept on your device only.

## iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress, settings and calculator history are synced between your own devices through your private iCloud account (Apple CloudKit private database and iCloud key-value storage). This data is stored by Apple under your Apple ID and is not accessible to us. You can turn iCloud sync off for the app in the iOS Settings app at any time. See Apple's privacy policy: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.

## Screenshots, photos and the camera

- When you pick a screenshot or a photo of a poker table, the app reads the cards, stacks and pot **on your device** using Apple's on-device text and image recognition. The picture is not uploaded anywhere and is not stored by the app after it is read.
- The live camera mode, where available, also works **only on your device**. Camera frames are processed in memory and are not recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The app asks for camera permission before first use, and you can withdraw it in the iOS Settings app.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data leaves your device and no push service is used for reminders.

## Purchases

Poker Pal Pro subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only receives a confirmation from Apple that your Apple ID has an active subscription, and checks it on your device. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's privacy policy.

## Children

The app is not directed to children and is rated for adults by the App Store. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Your choices and rights

Because we do not collect personal data, we hold nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. You can delete all app data at any time: in the app (Settings → Reset), by removing the app, and by deleting the app's data from iCloud in the iOS Settings app (Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage).

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
